ECEN 482/882 Antennas and Propagation for Wireless Communications (UNL)

This course covers the fundamental theory, design principles and design tools use for antenna analysiss and design. Specifically designed metal and dielectric structures that can radiate an applied RF signal in the form of electromagnetic radiation which is central to all modern wireless communication systems. An exponential explosion in information transmission and a boom in information technology is bsed this connectivity of wireless devices through wireless links provided by a range of electromagnetic structures. This class broadly introduce antenna theory and dive into the design and applications of antennas. The course will use electromagnetic full-wave simulation tools (Altair-FEKO and/or Ansys HFSS). The course will use assignments which are based on using these simulation tools. There will be two midterm tests and one final project based in this course.

This course was offered in Fall-2024, 2025. It will be offered in Spring-2026.

ECEN 468/868 Microwave Engineering (UNL)

Microwave Engineering will cover fundamental theory and design of components used in wireless communication, optics and integrated circuits. These components are central to cellular communication, Wi-Fi / Bluetooth systems design, satellite communication, optics and integrated circuits. With an increasing clock speed in the GHz microprocessors, analysis of high frequency distributed effects has become significant, and must be considered in design and analysis. Moving away from low-frequency analysis of devices and systems, this course will introduce foundational concepts for systems in millimeter and microwave bands. Some examples are, analysis of two port network measurement using scattering matrices and ABCD matrices, concept of impedance transformation and matching using smith chart, group and phase velocity and dispersion in guided propagation. Using these concepts, the course will further introduce a diversity of microwave-components including microstrip lines, strip lines, microwave filters, power dividers, couplers, phase-shifters, circulators and resonators through basic design concepts and examples considered in the classroom. The course will also deliver design related topics through techniques such as impedance matching using smith chart, and matching through open/short stubs, LC component-based matching. Finally, the course will focus on measurement and characterization techniques using vector network analyzers by covering topics such as two port network measurement and noise. The course’s objective is to develop among students, a strong analysis and design skill in the area of microwave engineering for contributing towards research and technological innovation in key technology areas.

This course was offered in Fall-2024, 2025. It will be offered in Spring-2026.

ECEN 306 Circuits III

This course cover the basics of semiconductor materials, Doping in semiconductors, p-n junction, diodes, space charge region, forward and reverse operation, basic DC circuits using diodes and their applications, MOSFET basics on physical structure, operational models and IV curves, BJT Basics including physical structure, operational models and IV curves, MOSFET DC and small signal AC model, MOSFET DC and small signal AC model, MOSFET Amplifier configurations (“common gate”, “common source”, “common drain”), BJT Amplifier configurations (“common base”, “common emitter”, “common collector”) and frequency response of amplifiers.

This course was offered in Fall-2024, 2025. It will be offered in Spring-2026.

EEL 5482 Fields & Waves Engineering

Foundational concepts in electromagnetic wave-propagation for wireless communications. Principles of propagation via state-of-the art simulation analysis coupled with theory. Fundamentals of radio-wave propagation in different media (conductors, dielectrics) Different types of propagating-waves. Boundary conditions for wave-fields. Polarization of waves for wireless transmission and reception. Propagation effects of radio-signals in non-homogeneous media (practical analysis of reflection and refraction). Propagation of waves in practical RF transmission lines: microstrip-lines, waveguides, coaxial-cables, microstrip-lines and optical fibers. Radar cross-section analysis for radar systems and radar targets.

EEL 3396 Introduction to Solid State Electronics

Basics of semiconductor materials: lattice structure, formation of energy bands, effect of doping. Basics of semiconductor materials: Concept of effective mass, Density of States, Thermal Equilibrium and Fermi function, electron density calculation. Motion of electrons and holes: Drift ad Diffusion currents, scattering, conductivity, quasi fermi level. PN junction basics, PN junction diode, junction breakdown, tunneling and avalanche breakdown. Optoelectronic Devices: Solar cells, LEDs and Solid-State lighting, Lasers, Photodiodes. Metal-Semiconductor Junctions: Schottky Barriers and Ohmic Contacts. Metal -Oxide-Semiconductor (MOS) capacitor: Surface accumulation, inversion and depletion conditions in MOS capacitor. Metal-Oxide-Semiconductor (MOS) Transistor, I-V Characteristics and Small Signal model. Bipolar Junction Transistors (BJTs) introduction and basic operation.
